      Is the walkthrough free?
      A video walkthrough is free. In person, a mileage fee may apply depending on distance. After it, you get a written quote that is good for 14 days.
      What work do you not take on?
      Whether we take on mold, sewage, needles, hoarding, or chemical spills depends on what we see at the walkthrough. If we find a hazard we can safely handle, it's $150 per hazard, added to your bill. If a hazard means we have to stop the job, the deposit is not refunded, and you pay a prorated fee for the work already done.
      How long is a quote valid?
      Quotes are valid for 14 days.
      How are dump fees billed on a cleanout?
      Dump fees are a pass-through: the dump receipt plus 10%. We can’t give a fixed dump price ahead of time because it comes from the receipt, so it’s added to your bill after the dump run.
      What if I cancel or you can’t get in?
      If you cancel with less than 24 hours’ notice, the charge is 50% of the job. If our crew arrives and can’t get in (a lockout), the charge is 100% of the job.
