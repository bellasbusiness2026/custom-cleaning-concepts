2. Liability Release Clauses

Clause 1: Force Majeure & Unforeseen Events Beyond Provider Control

CCC, its technicians, contractors, and employees shall not be held liable or responsible for failure to perform, service delays, or property damage resulting from events beyond its reasonable control. These events include, but are not limited to: severe weather, flash flooding, hurricanes, winter storms, regional utility power outages, municipal water shut-offs, road closures, acts of God, civil disturbances, pandemics, unexpected medical emergencies of crew members, sudden vehicle/equipment mechanical failures, or third-party criminal acts and vandalism occurring at the property site.

Clause 2: Pre-Existing Damage, Wear-and-Tear & Hidden Structural Defects

CCC is not liable for pre-existing deterioration, latent defects, or wear-and-tear encountered during standard operations. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Loose, improperly mounted, or unanchored wall shelves, mirrors, artwork, or towel bars.
  • Aged, sun-brittle, or deteriorating window blinds, cords, and shades.
  • Degraded, cracked, or missing grout and tile caulking.
  • Etched, unsealed, or porous natural stone, granite, or marble surfaces.
  • Pre-existing scratches, scuffs, or worn sealants on hardwood and vinyl flooring revealed during deep cleaning.
  • Concealed plumbing leaks, electrical shorts, or subfloor degradation behind appliances or cabinetry.

Clause 3: Unsecured Valuables, Fragile Items & Occupied/Shared Access

  • Securing Valuables: Client agrees to lock away and secure all cash, high-value jewelry, original art, heirlooms, firearms, and sensitive legal/financial documents prior to CCC’s arrival. CCC assumes zero financial or legal liability for claimed loss of unsecured, un-inventoried items.
  • Fragile Items: Items of extreme sentimental or monetary value must be identified to CCC in writing prior to service.
  • Shared Property Access: CCC accepts no liability for property loss, damage, or security breaches when third parties (including guests, realtors, general contractors, sub-contractors, or former tenants/occupants) hold active keys, lockbox codes, or access to the premises during the service period.

Clause 4: Exterior, Lawn Care & Underground/Concealed Hazards

During exterior maintenance, pressure washing, or lawn care services, CCC shall not be held responsible for:

  • Damage to unmarked, shallow, or improperly installed underground sprinkler heads, drip lines, or invisible pet containment fences.
  • Unmarked, exposed low-voltage utility lines, cable wiring, or shallow plumbing fixtures.
  • Damage caused by hidden debris, rocks, metal, or foreign objects obscured within overgrown grass, weeds, or heavy brush.
  • Pre-existing roof leaks or water intrusion caused by high-pressure washing on aged siding or compromised window seals.

Clause 5: Limitation of Financial Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, the total aggregate financial liability of Custom Cleaning Concepts (CCC) for any and all claims, losses, damages, legal costs, or injuries arising out of or related to any service rendered—whether in contract, tort (including negligence), or strict liability—shall be strictly limited to a maximum dollar amount not to exceed the actual total fees paid by the Client to CCC for that specific service visit. Under no circumstances shall CCC be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, special, punitive damages, or lost business profits, including lost short-term rental revenue or canceled guest bookings.
