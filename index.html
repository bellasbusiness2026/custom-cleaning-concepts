How booking works

The site is only for sending a request. This site never asks for payment.

  1. 1

    Send a request

    Tell us about the property and the work you need using the form below, or call or email.

  2. 2

    Same-day reply

    We reply the same day to requests received by 5 PM; later requests get a reply the next morning.

  3. 3

    Free walkthrough

    We walk through the property at no charge so the quote matches the actual work.

  4. 4

    Written quote

    You receive a written quote and agree to it in writing before the work begins. Quotes are valid for 14 days.

  5. 5

    Deposit and scheduling

    A 20% booking deposit is due after you agree to the quote. That deposit is non-refundable. We'll tell you how to pay the deposit with your written quote. Your date is held once the deposit is received. This site never asks for payment.

  6. 6

    The work

    The work happens as scheduled. The balance is due on completion.

Terms and common questions

What area do you serve?
Houston and nearby suburbs.
Are the prices on this page the final job price?
No. Posted prices are starting points. The starting price is not the final job price. The written quote you agree to is the price for the job.
How do booking and payment work?
Send a request. We reply the same day to requests received by 5 PM; later requests get a reply the next morning. Then we do a free walkthrough. You receive a written quote and agree to it in writing before the work begins. A 20% booking deposit is due after you agree to the quote; that deposit is non-refundable. We'll tell you how to pay the deposit with your written quote. Your date is held once the deposit is received. This site never asks for payment. The balance is due on completion. 1.5% a month on balances unpaid 15 days after completion.
What work do you not take on?
If we find a hazard we can safely handle, it's $150 per hazard, added to your bill. We don't take on mold, sewage, needles, hoarding or chemical spills. If a hazard means we have to stop the job, the deposit is not refunded, and you'll be charged a prorated fee for the work already done.
How long is a quote valid?
Quotes are valid for 14 days.
How are dump fees billed on a cleanout?
Dump fees are a pass-through: the dump receipt plus 10%.
