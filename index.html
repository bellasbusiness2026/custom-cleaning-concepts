      Terms and common questions

      What area do you serve?
      Houston, Pasadena, Webster, Clear Lake, Baybrook, Friendswood, the Heights, Downtown, Memorial, and Katy.
      Are the prices on this page the final job price?
      No. Posted prices are starting points. The price you agree to is the price for the job: your written quote, or the posted rate for the simple job you book directly. Handyman work is $70 an hour with a 2-hour minimum ($140).
      How do booking and payment work?
      Pick your services on this page, choose the date you’d like, and send your request. Requests received by 5 PM get a reply the same day. Later requests get a reply the following business day. For most jobs, we then do a free walkthrough, with no fee. The quote comes from that walkthrough: you get it in writing and sign it online before any work begins. After you sign, you go to a secure deposit payment page for the 20% booking deposit. Simple jobs can be booked directly instead: after you send your request, you get a request ID and can pay the deposit right away on a secure checkout page. Either way, you then confirm your receipt with your request ID, and once Lucille confirms your deposit, she sends you the link to choose your date and time. The deposit is non-refundable, and your date is held once Lucille confirms your deposit receipt. You can also pay the deposit with Venmo (see below). This site itself never takes your card details. The balance is due on completion. Balances unpaid 15 days after completion are charged 1.5% a month.
      Can I pay the deposit with Venmo?
      Yes. You can pay the 20% deposit with the secure card link, or with Venmo @ccc_htxowner_lucy (opens in a new tab). Wait until Lucille sends your quote or booking confirmation, because the deposit amount comes from it. Put your request ID in the Venmo note, along with your name or quote number, so we can match your payment. Then confirm your receipt: email or text Lucille your request ID and a screenshot of your Venmo payment. The deposit is non-refundable, and your date is held once Lucille confirms your deposit receipt.
      Is the date I pick on the form confirmed?
      No. It’s the date you’d like. We confirm your date with you, and it’s held once Lucille confirms your deposit receipt.
      What if something goes wrong with my booking or deposit?
      The form won’t send: use the simple request form under the booking form, or call or text 346-400-4656. You don’t see a request ID: don’t pay yet. Call or text and Lucille will give you one. You paid but lost your request ID: confirm your receipt anyway with the name and phone number you booked with. You paid but haven’t heard back: your date isn’t held until Lucille confirms your receipt, so if you haven’t heard from her within one business day, call or text 346-400-4656 or email ccc.htx.ownerop.lucy@gmail.com with your request ID and a screenshot of your receipt.
      Which jobs can I book directly?
      Single-item pickup, Airbnb turnovers, a lawn base visit (lots up to 3,000 sq ft), and handyman repair at $70 an hour with a 2-hour minimum ($140). These have a fixed posted rate. Everything else starts with a walkthrough and a written quote. For a direct booking, you pay the 20% deposit on a secure checkout page, confirm your receipt with your request ID, and then pick your date and time once Lucille confirms it.
      Is the walkthrough free?
      Yes. The walkthrough is free, with no fee. After it, you get a written quote that is good for 14 days.
      What work do you not take on?
      Whether we take on mold, sewage, needles, hoarding, or chemical spills depends on what we see at the walkthrough. If we find a hazard we can safely handle, it's $150 per hazard, added to your bill. If a hazard means we have to stop the job, the deposit is not refunded, and you pay a prorated fee for the work already done.
      How long is a quote valid?
      Quotes are valid for 14 days.
      How are dump fees billed on a cleanout?
      Dump fees are a pass-through: the dump receipt plus 10%. We can’t give a fixed dump price ahead of time because it comes from the receipt, so it’s added to your bill after the dump run.
      What if I cancel or you can’t get in?
      If you cancel with less than 24 hours’ notice, the charge is 50% of the job. If our crew arrives and can’t get in (a lockout), the charge is 100% of the job.
